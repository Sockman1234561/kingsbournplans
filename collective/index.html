Three quick questions

Answer one question at a time and the price builds up as you go. It takes about two minutes, and nothing is booked until we've talked it through together.

How many training sessions would you like?

Each session is 1.5 hours for you and your team, at the Collective or online. You learn by building real tools for the Collective, so the know-how stays with you. The minimum is four sessions.

4

If you also book a day with us (next question), we'll shape the sessions around what we see.

Would you like us to spend a day alongside you?

Pick a normal working day. We sit in on the real admin with you, lend a hand where we can, and see how the Collective actually runs.

What it gives you
  • Training shaped around how you and your team work
  • A shortlist of jobs worth automating, each with its price
  • The starting point for any build: we only build what we've seen will genuinely save you time
What builds cost

Builds are agreed after your day with us, so they aren't chosen here. The day comes first: it's how we make sure each build is worth doing, and you see the price before we start.

Everyday tool
One job, done reliably every time. For example, the monthly renewals and billing check.
Connected workflow
Several steps or tools working together. For example, website enquiries going straight into Capsule with a follow-up task.
Something bigger
Such as a booking and membership system. We estimate the hours and agree the price with you before starting.

Without a day with us we can't take on builds, but you can add one later.

Would you like ongoing support once things are running?

Short, regular check-ins once things are up and running.
